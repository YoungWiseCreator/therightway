What is a chavurah?

The word comes from chaver—friend. A chavurah is a small group, usually five to fifteen people, that gathers regularly in a home to walk the journey together: one chapter, one meal, one honest conversation at a time.

Start one

1. Find two or three friends. 2. Pick a weekly time and a home. 3. Open the Journey map where the channel is walking and read the chapter together. 4. End with one act of help for someone outside the circle. That is the whole seed— we will publish a free starter guide for new chavurot on the Blog.

Join one

Chavurot grow by invitation and by neighborhood. Start by gathering with the people already around your table, and bring friends from your congregation, school, or street. As registered chavurot open their doors, their gatherings will be listed here.

Outreach

Every circle carries the community outward: visiting the isolated, feeding neighbors, helping families find real assistance (see Get Help). Everything here stays free; donations help carry the cost of outreach like this.

New to the Hebrew path?

Many who walk with us are exploring the Hebrew roots of faith for the first time, and some ask about conversion and joining the Hebrew community formally. We are a study and storytelling community, not a congregation: we will point you honestly to local teachers, congregations, and established programs in your area, and we will walk the text with you while you find your place. Bring your questions to a chavurah—there is no wrong place to start.

Community partners

We work alongside Hebrew-focused organizations, educators, and community leaders whose work serves the same road, so families can find practical support and each other more easily.
