Community

No one walks the road alone

Etz Chaim is a community before it is a website. People from every background — every tradition, every starting point — gather here around the Hebrew Scriptures, share their perspectives, and learn from one another. The living room of that community is the chavurah (חָבוּרָה): a small circle of friends who meet in person to study, pray, eat, and look after their neighborhood.

What is a chavurah?

The word comes from chaver — friend. A chavurah is a small group, usually five to fifteen people, that gathers regularly in a home to walk the journey together: one chapter, one meal, one honest conversation at a time.

Start one

1. Find two or three friends. 2. Pick a weekly time and a home. 3. Open the Journey map where the channel is walking and read the chapter together. 4. End with one act of help for someone outside the circle. That is the whole seed — we will publish a free starter guide for new chavurot on the Blog.

Join one

Chavurot grow by invitation and by neighborhood. Start by gathering with the people already around your table, and bring friends from your congregation, school, or street. As registered chavurot open their doors, their gatherings will be listed here.

Outreach

Every circle carries the community outward: visiting the isolated, feeding neighbors, helping families find real assistance (see Get Help). Donated funds sponsor these outreach efforts directly.

New to the Hebrew path?

Many who walk with us are exploring the Hebrew roots of faith for the first time, and some ask about conversion and joining the Hebrew community formally. We are a study and storytelling community, not a congregation: we will point you honestly to local teachers, congregations, and established programs in your area, and we will walk the text with you while you find your place. Bring your questions to a chavurah — questions are the price of admission here.

Partners & sponsors

We build partnerships with Hebrew-focused organizations, educators, and community leaders whose work serves the same road, and we welcome sponsors whose offerings genuinely help this community. Partnership keeps the journey free and the gatherings funded — write to us through the channel and tell us what you have in mind.

Community runs on generosity

Donations host the gatherings: community events, chavurah outreach, and the meals where the real conversations happen.
