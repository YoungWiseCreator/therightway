Give the next generation a place to belong

Etz Chaim is built for young walkers too. The stories are told straight—and young people can handle straight. Around the episodes we run the challenges, events, and youth-led spaces that turn watching into walking.

Watch youth episodes Plan a family study night

Chapter challenges

Reading streaks, chapter races along the Journey map, and Hebrew-word challenges — earn milestones as you walk, book by book, with your chavurah cheering the count.

Game nights & events

Regular virtual game nights, sports challenges, and youth gatherings — online and in person — where the community's young people actually know each other's names.

Youth-led study

Young walkers lead their own study circles and services, with mentors beside them — presenting the chapter, leading the questions, and teaching what they have learned.

Where youth already are

Episodes and shorts live on The Right Way channel, cut for the feeds young people actually scroll — always pointing back to the full chapter.

Family Study Night Planner — free tool

Your kids' ages + this week's chapter → a one-page family plan: the story retold at their level, two discussion questions per age band, one activity, and the youth challenge tie-in. Done for you for free, no charge, no credit card.
