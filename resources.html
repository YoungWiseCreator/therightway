Resource Center

You do not have to carry this alone

If alcohol, drugs, tobacco, or gambling has a hold on you — or on someone you love — this page is a hand on your back, pointing at doors that are really open. Every program listed here is free, and every one was checked against its official source before it was allowed on this page. Each carries a plain step-by-step guide, written for a hard day: what the program is, what it costs (nothing), exactly how to start, and what to expect when you do.

We are a Bible community, not a treatment provider: we don’t run these programs and we can’t promise any program’s openings or outcomes. What we can promise is that the phone numbers and links below are the real ones, and that the first step at every one of them is free.

Start here if you don’t know where to start

Not sure which door is yours? These are for everybody, right now, whatever the struggle and whatever the hour:

SAMHSA National Helpline

The SAMHSA National Helpline (1-800-662-HELP) is free, confidential, 24/7 — referrals to local treatment for alcohol, drugs, and mental health.

Read the step-by-step guide

988

The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: free, confidential, 24/7 support for anyone in emotional distress, including distress tied to alcohol or drug use.

Read the step-by-step guide

FindTreatment.gov

FindTreatment.gov is SAMHSA's confidential, anonymous locator for substance-use and mental-health treatment facilities across the U.S.

Read the step-by-step guide

National Problem Gambling Helpline

The National Problem Gambling Helpline (1-800-MY-RESET) is free, confidential, 24/7 — help for sports betting, lottery, casino, slots, online gambling, and racing.

Read the step-by-step guide

In immediate danger — yours or someone else’s — call 911 first. In crisis or overwhelmed, call or text 988 — free, confidential, 24/7. Everything else can start with one call: 1-800-662-HELP (4357) for alcohol and drugs, or 1-800-MY-RESET (1-800-697-3738) for gambling.

Alcohol

Free rooms, free fellowships, and free places to live while you recover:

Alcoholics Anonymous

A.A. meetings are free everywhere: no dues, no fees, no sign-up. The only requirement for membership is a desire to stop drinking.

Read the step-by-step guide

Celebrate Recovery

Celebrate Recovery is a free, Christ-centered recovery program hosted by local churches. No dues, no fees, no registration — just show up.

Read the step-by-step guide

SMART Recovery

SMART Recovery meetings are free, in person and online: practical, science-based tools for overcoming alcohol, drug, and other addictive behaviors.

Read the step-by-step guide

Salvation Army Adult Rehabilitation

The Salvation Army's Adult Rehabilitation Centers are free, faith-based residential programs: housing, meals, counseling, and work therapy for about six months.

Read the step-by-step guide

Gambling

Sports betting, the lotto, the casino, the apps — every form of it. The free door is Gamblers Anonymous: no dues or fees, the only requirement a desire to stop gambling, with in-person, virtual, and phone meetings found at the official gamblersanonymous.org meeting search (questions: 855-2-CALL-GA). Gambling

Sports betting, the lottery, the casino, slots, online apps, the track — different games, the same hold, and the same door out. Whatever your form of gambling, the help below is for you: free helplines that answer day and night, free meetings, a room for the family, and the practical lock called self-exclusion:

National Problem Gambling Helpline

The National Problem Gambling Helpline (1-800-MY-RESET) is free, confidential, 24/7 — help for sports betting, lottery, casino, slots, online gambling, and racing.

Read the step-by-step guide

Gamblers Anonymous

GA meetings are free everywhere: no dues, no fees, no sign-up. The only requirement for membership is a desire to stop gambling.

Read the step-by-step guide

Self-Exclusion

Self-exclusion lets you ban yourself from casinos, sportsbooks, and gambling sites — free to enroll. How it works, and how to find your state's program.

Read the step-by-step guide

Florida’s Gambling Helpline

The Florida Council on Compulsive Gambling's 888-ADMIT-IT HelpLine: free, confidential, multilingual, 24/7 — call, text, or chat, for any kind of gambling.

Read the step-by-step guide

Self-Exclusion in Florida

Florida has no single statewide self-exclusion list. The real, free routes: 888-ADMIT-IT guidance, Seminole / Hard Rock exclusion including the sportsbook app, and the Lottery's own help door.

Read the step-by-step guide

Gam-Anon

Gam-Anon is a free fellowship for family and friends affected by someone else's gambling. No dues or fees — and they do not have to have stopped for you to come.

Read the step-by-step guide

Need food, rent, or benefits help too?

Recovery rarely travels alone — the bills and the empty fridge are part of the same hard season. Our Get Help page carries the practical doors: call 211 for local help today, Benefits.gov for what your household qualifies for, and Grants.gov for real grant listings. And bring the list 211 gives you to your chavurah — nobody in this community carries a hard season alone.

How this page is kept honest: a program appears here only after its official organization and its free offer are verified from the official source. If a program stops being free or its official door moves, it comes off this page. If any page ever asks you to pay to reach one of these programs, you are on the wrong page — come back here and use the official links in each guide.
