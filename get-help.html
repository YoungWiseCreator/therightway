Real help, from real programs

Faith and food belong on the same table. If you or your family need help right now—rent, utilities, food, benefits—these are the real government and community doors people use every day. They are free, they are official, and they answer.

Call 211 now Find recovery help

Call 211 — help near you, today

Dial 2-1-1 from any phone: a free, confidential line that connects you to rent help, utility assistance, food, and local programs in your own ZIP code. You can also search at 211.org, or text your ZIP code to 211 in many states. If the need is urgent, start here — 211 is faster than any application.

Benefits.gov — what you qualify for

Benefits.gov is the U.S. government's own benefits finder: answer a short set of questions and it lists the real programs — food, housing, healthcare, income — that fit your household.

Grants.gov — real grant listings

Real grants are listed in the open, on official pages — never behind a paywall. Grants.gov carries the federal listings; your state and county sites carry the local ones. A site charging you to "open" a grant list is selling you a search result.

Our sister sites — free tools, done with you

Empowerment Buddy decodes benefits — SNAP, Social Security, WIC — in plain words, with free tools and real people beside the AI. Grant Finder walks grants and assistance programs step by step. Both are free to use.

In crisis or overwhelmed — call or text 988

The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is free, confidential, and answers day and night — for emotional distress of any kind, including distress tied to alcohol or drug use. Call or text 988, or chat at 988lifeline.org. You do not have to be in the worst moment of your life to call; being in a hard one is enough. How 988 works →

Alcohol, drugs, tobacco, or gambling — the Resource Center

If the struggle is drinking, drugs, smoking, or gambling — sports, lotto, casino, online — start at our Resource Center: free helplines, free meetings, free quit coaching, and free residential programs — each one verified against its official source, each with a step-by-step guide. The SAMHSA National Helpline, 1-800-662-HELP (4357), is free and answers 24/7; for gambling, the National Problem Gambling Helpline, 1-800-MY-RESET (1-800-697-3738), answers around the clock too. The SAMHSA guide →

In immediate danger, call your local emergency number (911 in the U.S.) first. For everything else: call 211 today, and bring the list they give you to your chavurah — nobody in this community carries a hard season alone.
