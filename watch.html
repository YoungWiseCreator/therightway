Stories that play like a movie

Every chapter of the Bible becomes one episode of The Right Way — drawn characters acting the story in real time, in the tones and places the text itself describes, with narration at the opening, the ending, and the chosen beats that add explanation and reasoning. Nothing is added that the sources do not support.

The Right Way on YouTube Browse the chapter map

One chapter = one episode

At its natural length — most chapters run 6 to 12 minutes. A short chapter is a short episode; it is never stretched, and a long one is never cut down.

Sources on every episode

Each episode's description lists its sources: the oldest proven King James Version, the Hebrew Bible cross-translated alongside it, and the Dead Sea Scrolls where proven texts exist.

The three best sources on every chapter

On every chapter we bring the three best sources together to match — read the chapter in full, hear it read aloud, and watch the best tellings of it. Our own telling of each chapter is coming soon. The whole Bible in about thirteen months. Start at Genesis 1 and walk with us, or open the Journey map and join the road wherever you stand.

⚠️ Before every episode

These stories are presented raw, as written — and some of them are graphic. A short advisory opens every episode: we add no beliefs of our own; we depict from the Hebrew Bible and the King James Version, cross-checked against the Dead Sea Scrolls. Viewer discretion is advised.
