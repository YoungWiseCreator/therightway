What this is — in English

This section of the site is the Hebrew Bible, called the Tanakh in Hebrew: the same scriptures the King James Old Testament was translated from, presented in the original Hebrew, in the Hebrew Bible's own order. It has three parts: Torah (the Teaching), Nevi'im (the Prophets), and Ketuvim (the Writings) — traditionally counted as 24 books, covering the same 929 chapters as the Old Testament, arranged the Hebrew way. (A few chapter divisions differ slightly from English Bibles — Joel has four chapters here, Malachi three — the map below follows the Hebrew text itself.)

How a chapter page works: press Listen and hear the whole chapter read in Hebrew — in the same narrator voice you hear on The Right Way channel. Then Read the full Hebrew chapter, every verse, never a shortened version. Under the Hebrew you will find our new modern English translation of the same chapter — translated fresh from the Hebrew with today's tools, aiming at what the words actually mean — with its own English Listen player in the same voice. At the bottom, a Watch band shows the chapter on screen. Hebrew readers get the complete setup in Hebrew; English visitors get the complete setup too — nothing is shortened for either.

The text: the Westminster Leningrad Codex — the digital text of the oldest complete Hebrew Bible manuscript (the Leningrad Codex, copied in 1008) — public domain, distributed by eBible.org. The Hebrew is printed with its vowel points (niqqud), exactly as the manuscript tradition carries it.

Progress: 1 of 929 chapters in preview (Genesis 1). Chapters open in waves as each one passes — text complete, audio complete, watch band sourced.
