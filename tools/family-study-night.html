Family Study Night Planner

Twenty minutes, one chapter, the whole table. Tell us your kids' ages and the chapter your family is walking this week, and we build your one-page family study night plan: the story retold at their level, two discussion questions for each age band at your table, one hands-on activity, and this week's youth challenge.

Done for you for free — no charge, no credit card, ever. This is therightway.life: everything here is free, and donations are the only money-in this site ever asks for.

Your kids’ ages

Your result is built right here on this page the moment you submit — print it or save it. Your name and email go to us with your request so we can follow up and do this for you; they are used only for this request and are never sold or shared.

⚠️ The advisory travels with the plan

The Bible stories are presented as written, and some chapters are raw. Read your chapter yourself before family night and use your judgment for your own children — the plan retells at each age band's level, but you know your kids. When a chapter is heavy, the plan says so and shapes the night around the part a child can carry.

The plan's youth challenge ties into the Youth chapter challenges — bring your family's streak back there.
