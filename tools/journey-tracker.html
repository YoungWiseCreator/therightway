A 1,189-chapter road needs a way to mark where you stand. Tell us where you stopped and we build your personal Journey map: how many chapters are behind you, exactly where you are, and the next three episodes ahead of you — wired to the same 1,189 slots as the Journey map.

Done for you for free — no charge, no credit card, ever. This is therightway.life: everything here is free, and donations are the only money-in this site ever asks for.

Nobody is behind on this road; there is only where you stopped, and the next chapter.
