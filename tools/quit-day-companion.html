Quit-Day Companion Plan

The first meeting is a door. The thirty days after it are the road — and they are when most people walk it alone. This tool builds your personal first-30-days plan around your quit date: which days carry a meeting, a short check-in prompt for every single day, and the crisis lines set down in advance at the days that are known to be hard, so they are already in your hand before the hard day arrives.

Done for you for free — no charge, no credit card, ever. This is therightway.life: everything here is free, and donations are the only money-in this site ever asks for.

Your result is built right here on this page the moment you submit — print it or save it. Your name and email go to us with your request so we can follow up and do this for you; they are used only for this request and are never sold or shared.

If this moment is heavier than a plan can hold — call or text 988 (free, confidential, 24/7), or call SAMHSA at 1-800-662-HELP (4357). In immediate danger, call 911 first.
General support only: this plan is a companion, not medical advice and not treatment. It carries no diagnosis and no promises. If quitting could be physically dangerous for you — heavy daily drinking or drug use can be — talk to a doctor or call SAMHSA at 1-800-662-HELP (4357) before your quit date, and keep the crisis lines in the plan close for the whole 30 days.

Pair this plan with the Recovery Meeting & Class Finder to put real meetings, near your ZIP, on the days this plan calls for.
