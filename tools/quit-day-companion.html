Quit-Day Companion Plan

Tell us what you are quitting and your quit date. We build a dated 30-day plan with meeting days, daily prompts, and crisis lines ready for hard days.

Free from start to finish—no charge or credit card. Your result appears here.

How your details are used

Your result is built right here as soon as you submit. Your name and email also reach us with your request so we can follow up and do this for you; they are used only for this request and are never sold or shared.

If this moment is heavier than a plan can hold — call or text 988 (free, confidential, 24/7), or call SAMHSA at 1-800-662-HELP (4357). In immediate danger, call 911 first.
General support only: this plan is a companion, not medical advice and not treatment. It carries no diagnosis and no promises. If quitting could be physically dangerous for you — heavy daily drinking or drug use can be — talk to a doctor or call SAMHSA at 1-800-662-HELP (4357) before your quit date, and keep the crisis lines in the plan close for the whole 30 days.

Pair this plan with the Recovery Meeting & Class Finder to put real meetings, near your ZIP, on the days this plan calls for.
