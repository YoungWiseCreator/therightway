Florida Quitline — Free Coaching & Free Nicotine Replacement

Floridians get one of the strongest free quit offers in the country: a coach who answers day and night, and free patches, gum, or lozenges to take the edge off the first weeks.

What it is

The Florida Quitline is part of Tobacco Free Florida’s “Quit Your Way” program, run for the Florida Department of Health. It offers free, confidential quit coaching by phone — plus online enrollment and group options — for Florida residents who use tobacco.

Who it is for

Florida residents who want to quit tobacco. The free nicotine replacement offer is for adults 18 and older; coaching is there for every Floridian who calls.

What it costs

Free. Coaching is free around the clock, in English and Spanish. Eligible Florida adults can receive up to four weeks of free nicotine patches, gum, or lozenges — no prescription and no insurance required.

How to start — step by step

  1. Call 1-877-U-CAN-NOW (1-877-822-6669) — free coaching, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, in English and Spanish.
  2. Prefer online? Enroll at tobaccofreeflorida.com through the Web Quit program.
  3. Tell your coach how you use tobacco, and together pick a quit date and a plan.
  4. Ask about the free nicotine replacement — patches, gum, or lozenges, up to four weeks’ worth for eligible adults — and how it will reach you.
  5. Keep the follow-up appointments. Your coach checks in along the way; if you slip, call back — that is what the line is for.

What to expect at the first step

A real coach, a personal quit plan, and — if you qualify — quit aids mailed to you, all at no cost. No insurance card, no prescription, no bill later. The program is a public health service of the State of Florida, not a sales funnel.

Official door: Call 1-877-822-6669, tobaccofreeflorida.com
