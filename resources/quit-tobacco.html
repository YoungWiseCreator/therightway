Quit Tobacco Free — 1-800-QUIT-NOW & Smokefree.gov

Quitting tobacco is one of the hardest free things a person can do — so take every free hand offered: a personal quit coach by phone, and a written plan with daily support.

Start now

Call 1-800-QUIT-NOW (1-800-784-8669) · smokefree.gov · Text QUITNOW to 333888

How to start — step by step

  1. Call 1-800-QUIT-NOW (1-800-784-8669) — you will be connected to your state’s quitline. (Spanish: 1-855-335-3569.)
  2. Talk with your quit coach. Together you will set a quit date and a plan for your triggers and hardest hours.
  3. Ask what free support your state provides through the quitline — patches, gum, or lozenges are included for callers in many states.
  4. Build your written plan at smokefree.gov — “Create a Quit Plan” takes a few minutes and gives your quit a date and a shape.
  5. Add daily text support: text QUITNOW to 333888 for encouragement, advice, and tips on your phone.
  6. In Florida? You also have your own free state program with free nicotine replacement — see the Florida Quitline guide.

What it is

Two free federal doors. 1-800-QUIT-NOW is a toll-free number run by the National Cancer Institute that connects you to your own state’s tobacco quitline for free coaching. Smokefree.gov, also from the NCI, gives you a free personalized quit plan, text-message programs, and apps built by cessation experts.

Who it is for

Anyone who smokes, vapes, or uses tobacco and wants to stop — first attempt or fifteenth. Quitlines exist for exactly the person who has tried before; that is most of the people they help.

What it costs

Free. Quitline coaching is free, Smokefree’s plans and text programs are free, and many state quitlines also provide free nicotine patches or gum to enrolled callers — ask your coach what your state provides.

What to expect at the first step

Coaches are trained, patient, and famously non-scolding — slips are treated as information, not failure, and you can call back as many times as the road takes. A first call usually runs 10 to 30 minutes, and follow-up coaching calls are scheduled around your quit date.

If this moment is heavier than a guide can hold — if you feel you might harm yourself — call or text 988 right now. The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is free, confidential, and answers 24 hours a day. About 988 →
