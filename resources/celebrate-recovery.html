Celebrate Recovery — Free Church-Based Recovery, No Sign-Up

A Christ-centered recovery family that meets in churches everywhere — for alcohol, drugs, and every other hurt, hang-up, and habit. Their welcome is famous for a reason: you don’t even have to tell anyone you’re coming.

Start now

Find a group: locator.crgroups.info · celebraterecovery.com

How to start — step by step

  1. Go to celebraterecovery.com and open the Group Finder (“Find a Group”).
  2. Enter your city or ZIP code to find a local Celebrate Recovery ministry and its meeting night and time.
  3. Just show up. In the program’s own words: “You don’t need to sign up, register ahead of time, or even let anyone know you’re coming.”
  4. Walk in — greeters will point you to the large-group room. Sit anywhere.
  5. Stay for the open share groups afterward if you’d like. Listening only is always allowed.

What it is

Celebrate Recovery is a Christ-centered program, hosted by local churches, where people find freedom from hurts, hang-ups, and habits — including alcohol and drug addiction. A typical night is a large-group time of worship and teaching or testimony, followed by gender-specific open share groups, all built on the Twelve Steps and their biblical comparisons.

Who it is for

Anyone. You do not need to belong to the church that hosts it, or to any church. If you are working on a hurt, a hang-up, or a habit — addiction included — there is a seat with your name on it.

What it costs

Free. There are no dues or fees required to attend a Celebrate Recovery meeting. (Workbooks used in separate step-study groups are sold at cost by some groups — the weekly meetings themselves are free, full stop.)

What to expect at the first step

Loving people who will not judge you or try to fix you — again, their own description of their rooms. Worship, a teaching or a testimony, then small groups where you can share as much or as little as you choose. Come as you are is not a slogan here; it is the operating instructions.
