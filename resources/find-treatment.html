FindTreatment.gov — Search Treatment Near You, Anonymously

If you would rather look quietly on your own screen before you talk to anyone, this is the federal government’s own search tool — confidential, anonymous, and free.

What it is

FindTreatment.gov is run by SAMHSA, the U.S. Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration. It is a confidential and anonymous source of information about treatment facilities across the United States for substance use and mental health — searchable by location, facility type, and payment options.

Who it is for

Anyone looking for treatment options near home — for yourself or for someone you love — including people looking specifically for facilities with free, sliding-scale, or reduced-cost care.

What it costs

Free. The search is free, and it asks for no personal information — no name, no account, no sign-up.

How to start — step by step

  1. Go to findtreatment.gov.
  2. Enter your city, state, or ZIP code.
  3. Use the filters to narrow by facility type and by payment options — look for facilities listing free, sliding-scale, or government-funded care if cost is the wall in front of you.
  4. Open a facility’s listing and read what it offers, who it serves, and how intake works.
  5. Call the facility directly. Ask plainly: what does it cost, is there a waiting list, and what should I bring? Asking is free, and they answer these questions all day.
  6. If searching feels like too much right now, let a person do it with you: call the SAMHSA Helpline, 1-800-662-HELP.

What to expect at the first step

No account and no personal information are required — the search itself is anonymous. What you will find is real facilities with real phone numbers. The next step after searching is a phone call, and that call is the door actually opening.

Official door: findtreatment.gov, Questions: SAMHSA Helpline 1-800-662-HELP (4357)
