Self-Exclusion — Lock the Door Behind You, On Purpose

Willpower is a fair-weather friend. Self-exclusion is a lock: you sign up, in a clear moment, to be turned away in the weak ones — from the casino floor, the sportsbook app, or both.

What it is

Self-exclusion is a formal, voluntary agreement: you ask a gambling operator or your state to bar you from gambling with them for a set period — commonly six months to five years, sometimes lifetime. Many states run a single statewide program that covers their licensed casinos and sportsbooks at once. Online books also offer self-exclusion, deposit limits, and time-outs inside the app, and land-based operators run their own programs. Enrolling is free.

Who it is for

Anyone who wants the decision taken out of their own hands for a while — whatever your game: sports, slots, tables, online, the track.

What it costs

Free. State programs and operator self-exclusion do not charge to enroll. Know the teeth, though: under most programs, if you gamble while excluded, winnings can be forfeited. The lock is built to be taken seriously — by you, in this clear moment, most of all.

How to start — step by step

  1. Start with your state. Go to ncpgambling.org — Help & Treatment and use the state-by-state map there to find your state’s problem gambling program and its self-exclusion route.
  2. If your state runs a statewide program, follow its enrollment steps exactly — many let you enroll by form, by mail, or in person, with a photo ID.
  3. For online sportsbooks and casino apps: open the app, find Responsible Gaming in the menu or account settings, and choose self-exclusion. Set deposit limits and a time-out while you are there. Every licensed book is required to offer these tools.
  4. For a local casino, cardroom, or racetrack with no state program over it: ask the security or guest-services desk about voluntary self-exclusion. Most land-based operators run their own program — land-based terms commonly run from six months to lifetime.
  5. Close the side doors too: ask your bank or card issuer about blocking gambling transactions, and ask your state helpline about blocking software and cash-access tools.
  6. Tell someone. A lock works better when a person who loves you knows it exists — and if you want a human to walk the options with you first, call 1-800-MY-RESET.

What to expect at the first step

Enrollment is usually quick and private. Once active, the operator must refuse your play and take you off its marketing lists. Self-exclusion is not a force field — it will not reach offshore websites or a friend’s account — but it is a serious obstacle placed by your clearest self in front of your weakest moment, and for many people it is the step that finally holds.

Official door: Find your state: ncpgambling.org — Help & Treatment, Talk it through: 1-800-MY-RESET (1-800-697-3738)
