SMART Recovery — Free, Science-Based Recovery Meetings

If you want recovery built on practical tools and psychology rather than the Twelve Steps — or you want it alongside them — SMART Recovery’s free meetings may be your room.

What it is

SMART Recovery (Self-Management and Recovery Training) is a free recovery program grounded in science — cognitive behavioral therapy and motivational psychology — organized as a 4-Point Program: building motivation, coping with urges, managing thoughts and feelings, and leading a balanced life. Meetings are discussion groups led by trained facilitators, in person and online, in more than 20 countries.

Who it is for

Anyone working to overcome addictive behaviors — alcohol, drugs, and other substances or habits. There are also free Family & Friends meetings for the people who love them. No labels are required or used.

What it costs

Free. Meetings are free to attend, in person and online. Donations are requested to keep the program running, but they are never required — SMART states this plainly in its own support answers.

How to start — step by step

  1. Go to the meeting finder — SMART calls it the SMARTfinder — at meetings.smartrecovery.org.
  2. Enter your location for in-person meetings near you, or choose an online meeting at a time that fits — online meetings run around the clock across time zones.
  3. For an online meeting, follow the join instructions on the listing and come in at the start time.
  4. Just show up. Most meetings need no registration and no referral — walk in, or log in, and take a seat.
  5. Worksheets and tools used in the meetings are provided free; the handbook is optional and never required to attend.

What to expect at the first step

A discussion meeting: people talk with one another, not at one another, and the facilitator teaches concrete tools you can use the same day. There are no sponsors, no labels like “addict,” and no expectation of lifetime attendance — stay for as long as it serves you.
