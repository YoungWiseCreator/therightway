Gam-Anon — Free Support When Someone You Love Gambles

His bets. Her scratch-offs. The rent money that is not there. If someone else’s gambling is hurting your life, Gam-Anon is a room full of people who know exactly what that feels like — and it is for you, whether or not they ever stop.

What it is

Gam-Anon is a fellowship — separate from Gamblers Anonymous, and cooperating with it — for the spouses, partners, parents, children, relatives, and friends of compulsive gamblers. Working its own Twelve Steps, it helps members cope with the financial and emotional effects of a loved one’s gambling and rebuild their own steadiness. Meetings are held in person and virtually, and there is a national hotline.

Who it is for

Anyone who has been affected by someone with a gambling problem — that is Gam-Anon’s only requirement for attending. The person who gambles does not need to be in GA, or even willing to stop, for you to walk in.

What it costs

Free. There are no dues or fees for Gam-Anon. Members contribute voluntarily, as they are able, toward the cost of the room and literature — it is never a condition of walking in.

How to start — step by step

  1. Go to gam-anon.org and open the meeting directory.
  2. Choose the U.S., international, or virtual meeting list, and find a meeting near you or online.
  3. Just show up. There is no sign-up, and nothing you need to bring, prove, or prepare.
  4. Prefer to talk to a person first? Call the Gam-Anon Hotline: 844-475-4213.
  5. You can also sit in on an “open” Gamblers Anonymous meeting — GA listings mark which meetings welcome family and friends — to hear the other side of the room.

What to expect at the first step

People who have lived your exact situation — the hidden debts, the broken promises, the fear at the mailbox — sharing what helped them cope, set boundaries, and steady their own finances and hearts. You may simply listen, for as many meetings as you need.
