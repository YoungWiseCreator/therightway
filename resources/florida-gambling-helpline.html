Florida’s Gambling Helpline — 888-ADMIT-IT, Free and 24/7

Floridians have their own front door: a free helpline run by the Florida Council on Compulsive Gambling, answering around the clock, in English, Spanish, and more — for the sports bettor, the lotto player, and everyone in between.

What it is

The Florida Council on Compulsive Gambling (FCCG) is Florida’s nonprofit council on problem gambling, at work since 1988. Its 888-ADMIT-IT HelpLine is the problem gambling helpline dedicated to serving Floridians — free, confidential, and multilingual, reachable by call, text, live chat, and email. It connects callers to treatment referrals, Gamblers Anonymous meetings, self-exclusion resources, and the Council’s own free programs — for gamblers and for the people who love them.

Who it is for

Any Floridian concerned about gambling — their own or a loved one’s — whatever the form: sports betting, lottery and scratch-offs, casinos and cardrooms, slots, online play, or the track.

What it costs

Free. The HelpLine is free and confidential, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and it is the gateway to a series of free services the Council provides to Floridians. No insurance, no sign-up, no bill.

How to start — step by step

  1. Call or text 888-ADMIT-IT (888-236-4848) — any hour, any day.
  2. Prefer typing? Use the live chat at gamblinghelp.org, or email fccg@gamblinghelp.org.
  3. Say what is happening, in your own words. The specialists handle every form of gambling — and calls from family members are welcome too.
  4. Ask for the specific help you want: treatment referrals, GA meetings near you, self-exclusion options for where you play, or the Council’s free programs and materials.
  5. Take the referral, and make the next call the same day. Momentum is a form of mercy.

What to expect at the first step

A trained specialist who knows Florida’s gambling landscape and its help landscape. Confidential and multilingual. Thousands of people contact this helpline every year about every kind of gambling there is — your story will not be the first one like it they have heard today.

Official door: Call or text 888-ADMIT-IT (888-236-4848), Chat: gamblinghelp.org, Email: fccg@gamblinghelp.org
