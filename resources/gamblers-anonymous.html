Gamblers Anonymous — Free Meetings for Every Kind of Gambler

Betting slips, lottery tickets, slot machines, poker, the ponies, the app on your phone — GA rooms hold every kind of gambler there is, and the door is the same for all of them: just show up.

What it is

Gamblers Anonymous is a fellowship of people who share their experience, strength, and hope with each other so they may solve their common problem and help others recover from a gambling problem. Meetings — in person, virtual, and by telephone — are built around the Twelve Steps and held in communities all over the world.

Who it is for

Anyone with a desire to stop gambling. That desire is GA’s only requirement for membership — sports bettors, lotto players, casino and online gamblers alike. You do not have to have stopped already, and nobody at the door will test you.

What it costs

Free. In GA’s own words on its own site: “No dues or fees. Just show up, you will be welcomed.” A basket may be passed for voluntary contributions to cover the room; a newcomer is never expected to give.

How to start — step by step

  1. Go to gamblersanonymous.org and open the meeting finder.
  2. Choose the list that fits you — U.S. in-person meetings, international meetings, virtual meetings, or telephone meetings — and search by your city, state, or ZIP code.
  3. Pick a meeting. “Closed” meetings are for people who want to stop gambling; “open” meetings also welcome family and friends.
  4. Just show up, a few minutes early. There is no sign-up, no registration, no referral, and no paperwork — walk in and take a seat.
  5. Can’t find a meeting that fits? Call the GA National Hotline, 855-222-5542, and they will help you find one.

What to expect at the first step

First names only. You will not be asked to speak, pay, sign anything, or explain yourself — listening is a complete way to attend. You will hear people whose game was the same as yours, and people whose wasn’t, telling the truth about where gambling took them and how they stopped.

Official door: Find meetings: gamblersanonymous.org, GA National Hotline: 855-222-5542
