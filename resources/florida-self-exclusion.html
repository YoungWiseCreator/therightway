Self-Exclusion in Florida — The Doors That Actually Exist

Straight talk first: Florida has no one list that bans you from every kind of gambling at once. But the doors below are real and free, and they cover the biggest rooms in the state — here is how each one works.

What it is

Three working routes. (1) The Florida Council on Compulsive Gambling provides self-exclusion resources through its free 888-ADMIT-IT HelpLine — land-based operator programs (terms commonly run from six months to lifetime), online operator exclusion, and cash-access restrictions — and will walk you to the right one for your game. (2) The Seminole Tribe of Florida runs a Voluntary Self-Exclusion Program covering all of its Florida casinos and the Hard Rock Bet mobile sportsbook. (3) For lottery play, the Florida Lottery’s own Play Responsibly program points players to Florida’s dedicated problem gambling helpline, 1-833-PLAYWISE.

Who it is for

Floridians who want to be barred from the places and apps where they gamble — casino, cardroom, sportsbook, racetrack, or all of them.

What it costs

Free. Every route below is free to enroll, and every call is free.

How to start — step by step

  1. Call 888-ADMIT-IT (888-236-4848) first. The Council’s self-exclusion resources are provided through the HelpLine — tell them where you gamble (casino, cardroom, online, lottery) and they will direct you to the program that covers it.
  2. For Seminole casinos and Hard Rock Bet: download the Voluntary Self-Exclusion Program form from PlayersEdge (playersedge.org), Hard Rock’s official responsible-gaming program.
  3. Fill out and sign the form, then take it to the security office of any Seminole casino. The exclusion covers all gaming at every Seminole Tribe of Florida casino — including mobile sports betting on Hard Rock Bet.
  4. For other Florida cardrooms, racetracks, and slot facilities: ask the facility’s security desk about its own voluntary self-exclusion — or let 888-ADMIT-IT direct you. Land-based operators run their own programs; the state gaming commission’s exclusion list, by contrast, is one the regulator imposes, not one you join.
  5. For the lottery: the Florida Lottery does not run a ticket self-exclusion sign-up. Its Play Responsibly door is Florida’s dedicated problem gambling helpline — call or text 1-833-PLAYWISE (1-833-752-9947) — and it pairs well with Gamblers Anonymous and the steps in our general self-exclusion guide.
  6. Whatever you enroll in, write down the length of the exclusion and keep the paperwork somewhere safe.

What to expect at the first step

Honest limits: no Florida enrollment closes every door. The Seminole program covers Seminole properties only, and no list reaches offshore websites. That is why the helplines and GA sit beside the locks on this page: a lock plus a room full of people is stronger than either one alone.
