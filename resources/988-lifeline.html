988 — Call or Text When the Moment Is Heavy

Some moments are too heavy for a webpage. 988 is a free number staffed by trained counselors, day and night — and you do not have to be at the very edge to use it.

What it is

The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is a national network of local crisis centers. Call or text 988 and you are connected to a trained counselor who listens, understands how your problems are affecting you, provides support, and connects you to resources. The Lifeline welcomes calls about substance use as well as emotional distress of every kind.

Who it is for

Anyone who is overwhelmed, in crisis, or worried about someone else. You do not have to be suicidal to call — the Lifeline states plainly that its counselors help with substance-use concerns too. If you are worried about a friend or family member, you can call for them as well.

What it costs

Free. Calling, texting, and chatting are free and confidential, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. No personal data is required to connect with a counselor.

How to start — step by step

  1. Call or text 988 — any phone, any hour.
  2. Veterans: call 988 and press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line. Spanish speakers: press 2, or text AYUDA to 988.
  3. Prefer typing? Chat online at 988lifeline.org.
  4. Say as much or as little as you can. There is no script you have to follow and no wrong first sentence.
  5. Stay on the line as long as you need. If you want it, the counselor can connect you to local help that continues after the call.

What to expect at the first step

A trained counselor, not a recording and not a call center script. Confidential and judgment-free, in English and Spanish, with interpreter services for more than 240 other languages. The call is about support in this moment — and a bridge to whatever help comes next.

Official door: Call or text 988, Chat: 988lifeline.org
