National Problem Gambling Helpline — One Free Call, Whatever Your Game

Sports book or scratch-off, casino floor or phone app, the track or the tables — the form of gambling does not matter here. One free call connects you to help in your own state, day or night.

Start now

Call or text 1-800-MY-RESET (1-800-697-3738)Chat: 1800myreset.orgncpgambling.org

How to start — step by step

  1. Call or text 1-800-MY-RESET (1-800-697-3738) — any time, day or night. (The helpline’s longtime number, 1-800-522-4700, still answers too — both reach the same network.)
  2. Prefer typing? Chat free and confidentially at 1800myreset.org.
  3. Tell the counselor, in your own words, what is going on — which kind of gambling, how often, and what is worrying you. There is no script you have to follow.
  4. They will connect you to help in your own state: treatment programs, support meetings like Gamblers Anonymous, and your state council’s resources.
  5. Write down the names and numbers they give you, and take the next step the same day, while the door is open in your heart.
  6. In Florida? Your state has its own dedicated helpline too — 888-ADMIT-IT. See the Florida gambling helpline guide.

What it is

The National Council on Problem Gambling (NCPG) operates the National Problem Gambling Helpline — a one-stop hub that connects people looking for help with a gambling problem to local resources, through contact centers covering all 50 states and U.S. territories. You can call it, text it, or chat with it online. It serves every form of gambling: sports betting, lottery, casino games, slots, online gambling, and horse racing.

Who it is for

Anyone worried about gambling — your own, or someone you love’s. You do not need to be sure it counts as “a problem,” you do not need insurance, and you do not need the right words. Wondering whether to call is reason enough to call.

What it costs

Free and confidential. The helpline is free, confidential, and available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — its own chat page says so in capital letters. Calling never signs you up for anything and never obligates you to anything.

What to expect at the first step

A trained counselor — not a sales pitch and not a lecture. Confidential, judgment-free, and free. The helpline is the front door: it gives support in the moment and referrals to the local help that continues after the call.
