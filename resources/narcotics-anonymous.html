Narcotics Anonymous — Free Meetings for Drug Recovery

NA is where people recovering from drug addiction meet, for free, in nearly every community on earth — and the door is the same everywhere: you just walk through it.

Start now

Find meetings: na.org — meeting search · Free app: NA Meeting Search

How to start — step by step

  1. Go to na.org and open the meeting search.
  2. Enter your location to see meetings near you — in person and virtual, with days, times, and formats.
  3. Or install the free official NA Meeting Search app (by NA World Services) and search from your phone.
  4. Choose any meeting and just show up a few minutes early — no registration, no referral, no paperwork.
  5. Take any open seat. If someone offers you a welcome packet or a phone list, that is the fellowship doing what it does.

What it is

Narcotics Anonymous is a global, community-based fellowship of people for whom drugs had become a major problem, meeting regularly to help each other stay clean. Meetings are free, held in person and virtually, and built around the Twelve Steps adapted from A.A.

Who it is for

Anyone who wants to stop using drugs. NA’s only membership requirement is a desire to stop using — the decision to join is yours alone, and nobody at the door will test you on it.

What it costs

Free. There are no dues or fees. NA is fully self-supporting through its own members’ voluntary contributions — a newcomer is never expected to give anything.

What to expect at the first step

First names only, and anonymity is the foundation of the whole fellowship. You will not be asked to speak, pay, or prove anything. You will hear people at every stage — some with days clean, some with decades — telling the truth in a room where the truth is normal.
