What “Etz Chaim” Means — the Tree of Life

Etz Chaim is Hebrew for Tree of Life. It is what Proverbs calls wisdom, and what tradition calls the Torah itself.

Etz Chaim — עֵץ חַיִים — means “Tree of Life.” The Book of Proverbs uses it for wisdom: “She is a tree of life to them that lay hold upon her.” Tradition also gives the name to the Torah itself, and to the wooden rollers a Torah scroll is wound upon — the handles the scroll is held by.

We chose the name because it describes exactly what this community does. A tree is alive; it grows in seasons; it feeds people who did not plant it. And you do not take hold of a tree all at once — you take hold of the branch in front of you. For us, that branch is the next chapter.

Gold for the divine, silver for the heavens, deep blue for the deep waters of the text, green for the living tree — the colors of this site are the name, drawn. Welcome to the shade of it.
