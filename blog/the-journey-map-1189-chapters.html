The Journey Map: 1,189 Chapters, One Road

66 books. 793,677 words. Zero chapters skipped. How the map of the whole walk was drawn — and how to use it.

Before a single episode was drawn, we counted the whole road. Every chapter of the King James text, word by word: 793,677 words, about 120 hours dramatized, and exactly 1,189 chapters — the longest, Psalm 119, runs twenty-two minutes; the average chapter runs about six.

That count set the covenant of this channel: one chapter, one episode, at its natural length. No chapter is too small to skip and none is padded to fill a slot. At three episodes a day, the full road takes about thirteen months — a year of walking, Genesis to Revelation, with nothing left out.

The map itself lives on the Journey page: every book opens to show its chapters, each with its length, and each slot becomes a link as its episode publishes. However far the channel has walked when you arrive, the map shows you exactly where the road is — and where to step in.
