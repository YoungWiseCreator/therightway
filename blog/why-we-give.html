Why We Give: What Your Donation Actually Does Here

No paywall, no tiers of truth. The journey is free because givers carry it. Here is exactly where a gift goes.

Everything on this site and this channel is free, and we intend to keep it that way — the widow and the student should walk the same road as the patron. That only works if some of the walkers carry the cost for the rest. So here is the whole ledger of intent, in the open.

Donations fund the journey itself — the research, art, and narration behind 1,189 episodes. They host community events and the meals where chavurot meet. They run youth activities: game nights, challenges, gatherings. And they sponsor outreach — real help carried to real families, the way our Get Help page describes.

When secure online giving opens on this site, every Donate button — and they are all over this site on purpose — leads to the same single giving link. Until then, the Donate page carries the full picture. Give when you can; walk either way.
