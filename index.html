Our mission

A Tree of Life to everyone who takes hold of it

Etz Chaim — the Tree of Life — is what the Hebrew tradition calls wisdom itself. This is a vibrant, Hebrew-centric spiritual community: a place to explore the Scriptures in their original soil, deepen your connection with the divine, and grow — through personal reflection, communal engagement, and stories told the way they were written. Whatever background you arrive from, there is a seat for you at this table.

Resource Center

Struggling with alcohol, drugs, tobacco, or gambling? Free help is real.

This community exists to walk people home — and some roads home pass through recovery. The Resource Center gathers every free program that actually answers: national helplines, free meetings in every town, free quit coaching, and free residential programs — each one checked against its official source, each with a plain step-by-step guide written for a hard day.
