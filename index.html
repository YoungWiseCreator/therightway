Our mission

A Tree of Life to everyone who takes hold of it

Etz Chaim — the Tree of Life — is what the Hebrew tradition calls wisdom itself. This is a vibrant, Hebrew-centric spiritual community: a place to explore the Scriptures in their original soil, deepen your connection with the divine, and grow — through personal reflection, communal engagement, and stories told the way they were written. Whatever background you arrive from, there is a seat for you at this table.

Our sources

Told from the oldest texts we can prove

Every episode is depicted from the oldest proven King James Version and the Hebrew Bible — combined and cross-translated — and cross-checked against the Dead Sea Scrolls where proven texts exist. We add no beliefs of our own, and every episode lists its sources. Read our full sources & advisory →

Resource Center

Struggling with alcohol, drugs, or tobacco? Free help is real.

This community exists to walk people home — and some roads home pass through recovery. Our Resource Center gathers the free programs that actually answer: national helplines, free meetings in every town, free quit coaching, and free residential programs — each one checked against its official source, each with a plain step-by-step guide written for a hard day.

Alcohol

Free meetings and fellowships — AA, Celebrate Recovery, SMART Recovery — and free residential programs. Start here →

Drugs

NA meetings near you, science-based SMART groups, and free places to live while you recover. Start here →

Tobacco

Free quit coaching by phone, free quit plans, and — for Floridians — free nicotine patches, gum, or lozenges. Start here →

Don’t know where to start?

One free call — 1-800-662-HELP (4357) — connects you to real help near you, day or night. Open the Resource Center →
