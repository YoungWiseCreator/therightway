What this is, and what it stands on

The Right Way is the channel; Etz Chaim — עֵץ חַיִים, the Tree of Life — is the community gathered around it at therightway.life. Together they carry one work: the whole Bible, chapter by chapter, in order, presented as written — for everyone, from every background.

Our sources

Every episode is built from three layers, in this order:

The oldest King James Version

The oldest proven KJV obtainable — the text of record for narration. Modern translations leave things out; the KJV is our authority for what is depicted.

The Hebrew Bible

The Hebrew text, combined and cross-translated with the KJV, so the full story stands with all its details — and the Hebrew words underneath the English stay in view.

The Dead Sea Scrolls

A cross-check layer wherever proven scroll texts exist — the oldest witnesses we have, consulted chapter by chapter, alongside per-chapter artifact research.

These three, and no others. The channel's plain-English explanation — the storytelling layer that makes the ancient text understandable — is our own work, and it never adds a fact the sources do not hold.

Our advisory — the stance of this house

Every episode opens with an advisory card and voice, kept whole: these stories are raw and graphic, exactly as written. We add no beliefs of our own. We depict from the Hebrew Bible and the King James Version, cross-checked against the Dead Sea Scrolls — and every episode's description lists its sources.

One chapter is one episode, at its natural length. No chapter is ever skipped, paired, or padded. That is the whole covenant of this channel — 1,189 chapters, every one.

The name

"It is a tree of life to those who take hold of it" — that is what the Book of Proverbs says of wisdom, and Etz Chaim is also the name tradition gives the Torah itself. We take hold of it the only honest way: from the beginning, in order, together.
