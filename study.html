Watching is the road; studying is walking it slowly. Alongside every episode we build study material from the same sources — the Hebrew words underneath the English, the places, the artifacts, and the questions the chapter itself raises.

Chapter devotionals

A short devotional for every chapter on the journey: the passage in plain words, one Hebrew word worth knowing, and one question to carry into your day.

Hebrew word studies

Words like רוּחַ (ruach — breath, wind, spirit) carry more than any single English word can. Our studies open them up, verse by verse.

Every chapter is researched against real, proven artifacts and places — the scrolls, stones, and sites that stand behind the text — and cited in the episode itself.
