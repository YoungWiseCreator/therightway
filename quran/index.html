What this is — in English

This section of the site is the Quran, the scripture of Islam, presented in the original Arabic. It is made of 114 chapters, called surahs, arranged roughly longest first — from Al-Baqarah (286 verses) down to the short closing surahs of a few lines each. Each verse is called an ayah (plural ayat); the full text holds 6,236 numbered ayat. Almost every surah opens with the line "In the name of God, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate" (the basmalah) — it is counted as the first verse of the opening surah, stands unnumbered at the head of the others, and is absent only from Surah 9, At-Tawbah, exactly as the Arabic text carries it.

How a surah page works: press Listen and hear the whole surah read in Arabic — in the same narrator voice you hear on The Right Way channel. Then Read the full Arabic surah, every ayah, never a shortened version. Under the Arabic you will find our new modern English translation of the same surah — translated fresh from the Arabic with today's tools, aiming at what the words actually mean — with its own English Listen player in the same voice. At the bottom, a Watch band shows the surah on screen. Arabic readers get the complete setup in Arabic; English visitors get the complete setup too — nothing is shortened for either. This section carries the text, the audio and the watch band only — no commentary is mixed in.

The text: the Tanzil Quran Text (Uthmani), version 1.1 — the Hafs reading in the Uthmani (Madinah Mushaf) script, verified character by character by the Tanzil Project and reproduced here word for word, unchanged. Source: Tanzil Project (Creative Commons Attribution 3.0).
