Surah 1 · Al-Fatihah · الفاتحة (The Opening)

The opening chapter of the Quran — seven verses, the most recited passage in Islam, placed at the head of the book and at the head of every prayer.

About this surah — in English

Al-Fatihah means "The Opening". It is a short prayer in two movements: praise of God — His mercy, His lordship over all the worlds, His mastery of the Day of Judgment — and then a single request: guide us along the straight path. Its first line is the basmalah, "In the name of God, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate", which here counts as verse 1 itself. Below, you can hear it in Arabic, read it in Arabic, and read our modern English translation of the same seven verses.

2ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ

3ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

4مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ

5إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ

6ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ

7صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ

Read in English — our new modern translation

Translated fresh from the Arabic above with modern tools, aiming at meaning accuracy. (Cross-checked against the Saheeh International and Pickthall translations; every place the readings fork — including why "God" stands where the older translations print "Allah" — is on record in the library plan.)

1In the name of God, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate.

2All praise belongs to God, Lord of all the worlds —

3the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate,

4Master of the Day of Judgment.

5You alone we worship, and You alone we turn to for help.

6Guide us along the straight path —

7the path of those You have blessed; not the path of those who earned Your anger, nor of those who went astray.
